# Pre-registration: Reward weights under deployed envelope (lowbw-lambda-env6-v1, 2026-10-08)
- Method: a copy of lowbw-lambda-ch-v2, three arms λ 0.4, 1.0, 0 (seed 1, same weight file) all with probing envelope v6 and a 5 kbps floor; constant 100, 200, 300, 400 kbit/s, 3 runs per cell, 36 total, run script v26, two container pairs in parallel; weight check seed_weight_check_v5 (relative threshold only).
- Criteria (identical to lowbw-lambda-ch-v2): primary endpoint D = λ 1.0 minus λ 0.4, equal-weight utility difference across four cells, non-inferiority bound -5.27; holds: lower bound greater than -5.27 and no safety line triggered (any cell lower by more than 10 points or all λ 1.0 runs invalid in any cell); direction only: D greater than -5.27 but lower bound not greater than -5.27; does not hold: D not greater than -5.27 or safety line triggered. Report only: λ 0 minus λ 0.4 and the broken-call count per arm.
- Prediction: the envelope rewrites most decisions at low capacity, so all three weights may avoid broken calls with similar utility, i.e. the influence of the weight shrinks behind the envelope (this weakens the significance of "the quality term keeps calls from breaking" in the deployed system; reported as is).

- Method: a copy of lowbw-lambda-ch-v2, with the decision function, conditions (constant 100, 200, 300, 400 kbit/s, source 320x240), rotation, intervals, bounds and safety line unchanged; three arms are the deployed recipe seed 1 λ 0.4 (ch.pt), 0.8 (ch_l08s1.pt), 0.6 (ch_l06s1.pt, CPU-equivalence check passed on gpu-server-1, the discriminating control correctly failed the gate, hash b72f8d01), all with a 5 kbps floor and no probing envelope; 3 runs per cell, 36 total; run script v28.
- Primary endpoint D: λ 0.8 minus λ 0.4, equal-weight utility difference over four cells; non-inferiority bound -5.27; holds: lower bound greater than -5.27 and safety line not triggered; direction only: D greater than -5.27 but lower bound not greater than -5.27; does not hold: D not greater than -5.27 or safety line triggered (any cell more than 10 points lower or all λ 0.8 runs invalid in any cell). Fewer than 3 usable cells judged inconclusive.
- Report only: λ 0.6 minus λ 0.4; per-arm broken-call count, utility, VMAF, video rate, floor triggers.
- Weight check: seed_weight_check_v4 (relative threshold only: the second-smallest error is at least 10 times the smallest).

# Pre-registration: Splitting the closed-loop lead into ramp-up and steady state (closedloop-steady-qoe-v1, 2026-10-08, measurement only)
- Method: only the runs of the five registered 720p closed-loop batches (hd720-closedloop-ch-v1, hd720-echo-attr-ch-v1, hd720-seeds-ch-v1, hd720-highbw-ch-v1, hd720-randloss-ch-v1) are read, deployed system against GCC every 200 milliseconds, using the runs judged valid in each batch; no new runs.
- Definitions (fixed before running): per-frame VMAF is aligned by decode time, with the first frame at time 0; the first 30 seconds are the ramp-up phase and the rest is the steady-state phase; within a unit, the difference of the two arm means is taken, with cells equally weighted; the 90% interval comes from 10000 resamples of runs within cells (seed 20261008), as in hd720_ch_eval_v1.judge. The same method is used for the share of time frozen and for the differences in mean and 95th-percentile queuing delay; the median time for each arm's video rate to reach 0.8 of the source's rate cap is also reported.
- Criterion: measurement only, no verdict on the effect; "holds" (a definition check) is recorded if the readings from all five batches are complete and the full-run VMAF difference agrees with each batch's registered reading.
